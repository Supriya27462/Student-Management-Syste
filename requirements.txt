Student Management System - Requirements

Language standard: C99
Compiler: GCC, Clang, or MinGW (any C99-compatible compiler)
External libraries: None
Operating system: Windows, Linux, or macOS with a C compiler

Compile example:
gcc -std=c99 -Wall -Wextra -pedantic student_management.c -o student_management

Runtime data file:
students.dat (created automatically in the program's working directory)
